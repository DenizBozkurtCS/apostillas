# A Book is the work, not the edition

A Book's identity is the work (Open Library "Work"), not a specific Edition/ISBN. Edition details (ISBN, cover, page count) are optional attributes on the Book. Tracking editions would give exact metadata but produce duplicate Books when the reader rereads or switches format, scattering Reads, Quotes, and Notes across them.

## Consequences

- Quote page numbers may not match across editions; the Edition recorded on the Book is the reference.
